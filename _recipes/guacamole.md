ingredients:
  - 4 large avocados
  - 1/2 red onion
  - 1 roma tomato
  - 2 Tbsp cilantro
  - juice of 1 lime
  - 2 tsp salt 

directions:
  - Remove avocado skins and pits, mash in bowl.
  - Dice onion, remove seeds and dice tomato, combine.
  - Juice lime and add to bowl with salt.
  - Mix all ingredients. Store in fridge until serving. Flavor will get better around an hour after making.
